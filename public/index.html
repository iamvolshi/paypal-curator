1 SNAP ON 2 RETRIEVE 3 PAY 4 AUDIT

1 SNAP ON

Curator snaps onto a messy folder and sorts it by what each file is actually about — never by its filename.

2 RETRIEVE

Click a subject. Every fact Curator states points back at the exact file, line or message it came from. Click a pointer to see the source. If it cannot point at a source, it says so — it never guesses.

Attach the data pool in Act 1 first, then pick a subject.

3 PAY

The agent pays an invoice — for real, in the PayPal sandbox. You approve it as the sandbox buyer, then the agent captures it. No approval, no money moves. Ever.

Checking whether the PayPal sandbox is connected…

4 AUDIT

The K-record: everything the agent believed, was allowed to do, was trusted with, and paid — by date. Old entries are never edited. When it was wrong, the correction is a new entry, and the wrong belief stays in the log.

The sheet changes. The notes never do.
